A post naming any of these coins (cashtag, name, or ticker in capitals) is priced at once, without a lookup. Each coin is identified on CoinGecko, and priced on the first venue with hourly bars whose pair is trading and within 5% of the coin's price (Binance, then Hyperliquid, OKX, Gate, then Hyperliquid spot: a pair quoted in a USD stablecoin with trades in the last 24 hours; last, a DEX pool found through the coin's own contract, with at least $100,000 of liquidity and current hourly candles). A ticker two coins share is never guessed: the owner picks the coin. Stablecoins, wrapped / staked copies and tokenized funds, gold and stocks are not coins here and are skipped; the top 100 is counted after them. A weekly re-check flags any venue that stops trading or drifts away from the coin's price; a venue is never swapped silently.
